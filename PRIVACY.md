# Privacy & Data Handling

## Data Minimization
NutriFlow only collects data that is absolutely necessary to provide personalized meal recommendations. We do not ask for:
- Exact weight or BMI
- Medical history
- Highly specific location data

## What We Collect
- **Dietary Preferences:** To filter out unsuitable recipes.
- **Allergies:** For safety filtering.
- **Pantry Inventory:** To suggest meals based on what you already own.
- **Feedback:** Likes and rejections on recipes to improve the engine.

## Third-Party Services
- **Authentication & Database:** Supabase (Data is encrypted at rest).
- **Analytics:** PostHog (Configured to be privacy-safe, stripping personal identifiers when possible).

## User Rights
Users have the ability to:
1. View all their saved data.
2. Update preferences at any time.
3. Permanently delete their account and all associated data via the Profile page.
